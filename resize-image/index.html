GOOD TO KNOW

Before you resize

Does my image leave my device?

No. The browser processes the image locally; the file is not uploaded to Fyloz.

What does aspect ratio mean?

It is the relationship between width and height. Keeping it checked preserves the image's proportions when you change one dimension.

Can I make an image larger?

Yes, but enlarging an image cannot restore detail that was not in the original and may make it look blurry.

Which output format should I choose?

JPG is useful for photos, PNG supports transparency, and WebP can offer smaller files. JPG output fills transparent areas with white.
