GOOD TO KNOW

Before you download

Does my image leave my device?

No. Compression happens locally in your browser; the image is not sent to Fyloz.

Will it always reach my target size?

The tool tries lower image quality and smaller dimensions. Very small targets may be impossible to reach while keeping a usable image, and we’ll tell you when that happens.

What format will I download?

The output is a JPEG image. Transparent areas in PNG or WebP files are filled with white, so check the preview before downloading.

Will the image look exactly the same?

Compression can reduce detail, and smaller dimensions reduce resolution. Always inspect the preview before using the file.
