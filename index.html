QUICK ANSWERS

Image compression FAQs

Does my image leave my device?

No. Compression happens locally in your browser; the image is not sent to a Fyloz application server.

Will it always reach my target size?

The tool tries lower image quality and smaller dimensions. Very small targets may be impossible to reach while keeping a usable image.

What format will I download?

The compressed output is JPEG. Transparent areas in PNG or WebP files are filled with white.

Will the image look exactly the same?

Compression can reduce detail, and smaller dimensions reduce resolution. Inspect the preview before using the file.

Can I set a custom file size?

Yes. Enter a maximum from 1 to 20,480 KB or choose one of the presets. The downloaded file will be at or below your selected limit when that target is achievable.

What if an upload is still rejected?

Check the required format, pixel dimensions, and photo rules as well as the file size. Our upload troubleshooting guide explains what to check next.
