GOOD TO KNOW

Rotate and flip FAQs

Does editing change my original file?

No. Fyloz creates a new downloadable copy and leaves the original file on your device unchanged.

What is the difference between rotate and flip?

Rotate turns the image. A horizontal flip mirrors left and right; a vertical flip mirrors top and bottom.

Can I keep a transparent background?

Choose PNG or WebP to preserve transparency. JPG has no transparency and uses a white background.

Are the edits uploaded?

No. The image is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to the Fyloz application server.
