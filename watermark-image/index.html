GOOD TO KNOW

Watermark FAQs

Does this change my original file?

No. Fyloz creates a separate output file and does not modify the original.

Can I move the watermark?

Choose one of five positions: four corners or the center. Preview the result before downloading.

Can I keep transparency?

PNG and WebP can preserve transparent areas. JPG has no transparency and uses a white background where needed.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The browser applies the watermark locally. The image is not uploaded to the Fyloz application server.
