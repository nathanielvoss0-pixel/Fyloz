Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Image processing

All five image tools and the PDF toolkit process selected files inside your browser. Files are not uploaded to a Fyloz application server by these tools. The PDF page loads processing libraries from public CDNs. Document contents are processed locally and are not uploaded to those CDNs by this site. The browser creates a temporary local preview and download file; these temporary browser URLs are revoked when they are no longer needed or when you leave the page.

Information and technical logs

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use these tools. The hosting provider may process technical connection data, such as IP address and request details, as part of serving this website. Please review the hosting provider's own privacy information for details about its infrastructure logs.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This version of Fyloz does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated to explain what those services collect and how they work.

Children and changes

Fyloz is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect information from children. This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
