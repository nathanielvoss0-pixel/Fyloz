Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Image processing

Fyloz processes the image you select inside your browser. The image is not uploaded to a Fyloz application server by the compression tool. The browser creates a temporary local preview and download file; these temporary browser URLs are revoked when they are no longer needed or when you leave the page.

Information and technical logs

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the image compressor. The hosting provider may process technical connection data, such as IP address and request details, as part of serving this website. Please review the hosting provider's own privacy information for details about its infrastructure logs.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This version of Fyloz does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated to explain what those services collect and how they work.

Children and changes

Fyloz is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect information from children. This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
