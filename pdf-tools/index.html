FAQ

PDF tool questions

Are my documents uploaded?

No. The tools process your selected files in your browser. External library scripts are loaded from public CDNs, but the document files are not sent to those CDNs by these tools.

Will PDF optimization always make a smaller file?

No. Re-saving may reduce some structural overhead, but the result can also be the same size or larger. It does not guarantee image recompression.

Can I split a PDF into several files?

Yes. Choose Split by page ranges and enter ranges separated by semicolons, such as 1-3; 4-6; 7-9.

Does it work for every PDF?

Some encrypted, damaged, unusually complex, or very large PDFs may not be supported. If a file fails, try another PDF reader or export a fresh copy.
